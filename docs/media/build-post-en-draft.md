SignLatch now converts structured supplier intent into an exact review snapshot and
proves that human authority does not survive material mutation. Our fixture harness
changes the artifact, recipient, field, policy finding, and intent independently. Every
change blocks the approval, and restoring the original value still requires reapproval.

The Foxit boundaries are deliberately separate: PDF preparation is reversible; eSign
dispatch is dispatcher-only, budgeted, idempotent, and disabled by default. Provider
delivery is not yet claimed as live-demonstrated. The current public-safe claim is the
fixture approval latch, backed by the dated digest in `evidence/approval-harness.json`.
